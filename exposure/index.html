Method: Baker et al., “Epidemic mitigation by statistical inference from contact tracing data,” PNAS 118 (2021). Update per contact: new = (1 − prior) · P · λ. Recovery: 1/7 per day. λ = 1 − e−dose. The dose adds a close-range term that falls with distance squared and a well-mixed room term (Wells–Riley). Flu survives in dry air for longer, so low humidity raises the room term (Noti et al. 2013).
